Cheapest Solana Burner

Token Studio's cheapest Solana burner lets you burn unwanted tokens and NFTs, close empty accounts, trim excess rent, and see the exact assets and estimated SOL recovery before your wallet signs anything.

TL;DR

Token Studio is built for mixed, large-scale wallet cleanup. It packs compatible operations into 4,096-byte Solana V1 transactions and charges 0.8% of estimated SOL recovery, with a 0.01 SOL safety fee cap per burn transaction. The complete current amounts are listed on the service fees page.

Burn what you want to clean. Trim what you want to keep.

Burn

Remove the asset or account

Permanently destroy the selected asset where its standard supports burning, and close eligible accounts to recover their remaining rent.

Trim

Recover only excess rent

Reclaim excess SOL from eligible Legacy token accounts while leaving the account and its asset intact. The account is refreshed immediately before execution. As Solana's new rent-reduction SIMD lowers account rent, existing accounts may hold more SOL than the new minimum; Trim reclaims that difference without burning the asset.

More cleanup in each wallet approval

Burn and Trim use Solana V1 transactions with a 4,096-byte envelope and limits of 64 addresses and 64 instructions. The exact asset mix determines how many operations fit.

Exact capacity instead of guesses

The allocator measures serialized bytes, accounts, instructions, fees, and safety overhead while building each transaction.

Transaction stacking

Prepare four transactions per wallet confirmation. Token Time Vault collectors can use 8x and 16x stacking for larger jobs.

Atomic fees

The service-fee transfer is an instruction inside the same transaction as the cleanup. If that transaction fails, all of its instructions fail together and Token Studio collects no service fee.

A recovery fee designed around the outcome

Burn and Trim fees are based on the SOL the operation is expected to restore, not the market value of the asset being removed.

Recovery is estimated from the account's data length and Solana rent requirement. If Token Studio cannot estimate the recovery safely, the percentage-based fee is zero. The 0.01 SOL cap limits the fee if an estimate is wrong or an account unexpectedly contains additional SOL.

What happens to valuable fungible tokens?

As part of the Burn fee structure, a fungible balance worth more than $0.01 may be transferred to Token Studio's fee wallet before its token account is closed instead of being irreversibly destroyed. Retaining eligible balances helps make the low 0.8% recovery fee possible and comes with an additional safety benefit: an accidentally burned valuable balance may be recoverable through support for up to 30 days.

The estimator checks the balance

If receiving that mint requires a new token account, its roughly 0.0014 SOL creation cost is included and the estimated recovery may become zero.

The token balance may move before closure

When this part of the fee structure applies, the balance transfer and account closure occur as instructions in the submitted transaction.

Accidental valuable burns can be reported

Contact Token Studio on Discord within 30 days and tag @admin. Return is a support safeguard, not a guarantee or custody service.

Cheapest Solana Burner questions

Can I preview how much SOL I will recover?

Yes. Token Studio estimates account-rent recovery, subtracts applicable service fees, and shows expected net SOL before execution. It uses zero for the percentage fee when recovery cannot be estimated safely.

Can I burn NFTs and tokens in the same batch?

Yes. A selection can contain multiple asset types. The allocator separates operations into valid transactions according to their programs, required accounts, size, and instruction limits.

Does Trim burn my token or close its account?

No. Trim only withdrws excess rent from an eligible Legacy token account. It keeps the token account and asset intact.

Are compressed NFT burns supported?

Yes. Both V1 and V2 compressed NFT burns are available, with small flat fees reflecting their different operations. Collector transactions remain free of Token Studio service fees.

Are failed burn transactions charged?

No Token Studio service fee is collected when the containing transaction fails. Solana or wallet behavior outside that transaction may still produce separate costs.

Is Token Studio a Sol Incinerator alternative?

Yes. See the decoded NFT burn comparison for measured recovery, fee, instruction, and transaction-size differences.
