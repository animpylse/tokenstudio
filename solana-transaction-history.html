Flight History

Your Solana Transaction History, Finally Searchable

Download a wallet's activity, turn raw instructions into a chronological story, combine precise filters, and move from a daily value change to the transaction that caused it.

TL;DR

Flight History downloads, stores, parses, and classifies Solana transactions on your device. Search the timeline, combine 17 modular filters with AND, OR, and NOT, inspect every transaction, or select a day on the graph to jump straight to its activity.

From signatures to a readable timeline

Instead of sending users from one signature to the next, Flight History groups wallet activity by day and explains the primary action, direction, time, and estimated value change.

Ask complicated questions in plain building blocks

Search works alongside three logical groups. Filters update the transaction list, graph, totals, and matching count together.

ANDEvery included condition must match
ORAny alternate condition may match
NOTMatching activity is excluded

Advanced properties also cover fee components, program names, wallet relationships, and transaction status. Numeric filters support comparisons and ranges; selection filters can match any or all chosen values. Dates can be multiple ranges. Spam and failed transactions have separate hide controls.

Solana transaction history questions

Can I search by wallet address or recipient?

Yes. Search and the Transfer Counterparty property can isolate activity involving an address. The result can be copied or opened in read-only wallet exploration.

Can filters change the graph?

Yes. The transaction list, graph, total value change, daily average, and matching count are calculated from the active result set.

Does Flight History show every transaction forever?

No. The current standard tier covers up to 16,000 transactions and 30 days; collector access covers up to 128,000 transactions and 90 days. Network availability and gaps can also affect coverage. Transactions exceeding the maximum timeframe are pruned periodically.

Is the value-change graph a tax P&L report?

No. It is wallet analysis based on available historical pricing. It should not be treated as authoritative tax or accounting data.

Does Token Studio upload my transaction database?

Flight History stores its parsed database locally on the device. Solana RPC and pricing sources are still contacted to fetch public blockchain and market data.
