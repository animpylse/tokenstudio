The Solana Control Surface
Elevate your control. Define your legacy.

Token Studio is an all-in-one Solana asset management toolkit. Think of it as Photoshop for Solana, with your wallet as the canvas. It aggregates your entire on-chain footprint into a unified view of all your wallets, tokens, and NFTs, enriched with pricing and performance data.
Create, edit, print, transfer, freeze, delegate, and burn assets at scale with powerful batch operations and advanced filtering.
Built for the Solana ecosystem, Token Studio integrates directly with the Seed Vault, Solflare, and Phantom wallets to provide a secure, non-custodial experience while keeping you in full control of your assets.
